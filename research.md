  <p class="entry__title">Human Oversight in AI-Assisted Decision-Making</p>
  <p class="entry__meta">Safe &amp; Trusted AI Lab, BITS Pilani &middot; 2026&ndash;present</p>
  <p>I'm studying how human reviewers use, question, and override AI-generated recommendations in public-sector decision workflows, using an experimental decision-support interface modeled on India's PM-JAY health-insurance review process. The goal is to understand how accuracy, confidence, and evidence-seeking change under different forms of AI assistance.</p>
