# Research

I'm interested in how AI systems shape human judgment once they become part of real decision-making processes. My current work focuses on human oversight in AI-assisted public-sector decision-making: how people interpret algorithmic recommendations, when they rely on or challenge them, and what meaningful human control looks like in practice.

## Current Work

### Human Oversight in AI-Assisted Decision-Making

*Safe & Trusted AI Lab, BITS Pilani · 2026–present*

I'm studying how human reviewers use, question, and override AI-generated recommendations in public-sector decision workflows. My current project is a mixed-methods study modeled on India's Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) health-insurance review process, combining stakeholder interviews, workflow analysis, and an experimental decision-support interface to study how different forms of AI assistance affect decision accuracy, confidence, evidence-seeking, and reliance.

### [IndicClaimVer](https://sites.google.com/view/indicclaimver2026)

*FIRE 2026 · 2026–present*

I'm working on multilingual claim verification across English, Hindi, Bengali, and code-mixed claims. Our system combines dense evidence retrieval, reranking, and transformer-based veracity prediction, with a particular focus on how retrieval quality affects downstream verification. I'm currently writing the system-description paper with my team at CSIS, BITS Pilani.
