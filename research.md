I'm interested in how AI systems shape human judgment and behavior once they become part of real decision-making processes. My current work focuses on how people interpret, use, question, and respond to AI-generated recommendations in institutional settings.

## Current Work

### Human Oversight in AI-Assisted Decision-Making

*Safe & Trusted AI Lab, BITS Pilani · 2026–present*

I'm conducting a mixed-methods study of human-AI decision-making modeled on India's Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) health-insurance review process. The project combines stakeholder interviews, workflow analysis, and an experimental decision-support interface to study how different forms of AI assistance affect decision accuracy, confidence, evidence-seeking, reliance, and intervention.

### [IndicClaimVer](YOUR_LINK_HERE)

*FIRE 2026 · 2026–present*

I'm working on multilingual claim verification across English, Hindi, Bengali, and code-mixed claims. Our system combines dense evidence retrieval, reranking, and transformer-based veracity prediction, with a particular focus on how retrieval quality affects downstream verification. I'm currently writing the system-description paper with my team at CSIS, BITS Pilani.
